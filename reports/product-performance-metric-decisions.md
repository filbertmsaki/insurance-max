# Product-performance metric definitions — decision draft

Date: 6 November 2026. Status: proposed for Finance, Underwriting and Claims review; no definitions are approved. This is step 1 only. It does not authorize changes to calculations or historical records. Repository observations refer to the current working tree, not verified production data. Validate applicable accounting and regulatory requirements with Finance and Compliance.

## Reporting framework proposed for approval

- Report by tenant and authorized branch/department/user scope; show original currency on every amount. Do not sum different currencies. If a consolidated currency is required, show the original amount and record the approved rate, source and date separately.
- Identify period start/end, reporting time zone, report cutoff, date generated and whether the report is a current operational view or a closed accounting-period view. Keep issued, paid, effective and posted dates separate.
- Show gross written premium, earned premium, collections, outstanding receivables, cancellations and refunds as separate metrics. Do not count quotations as written premium or an approval as payment.
- Keep corrections and reversals as linked movements. A past-period figure is unavailable or qualified if source history cannot reproduce it; do not infer it from today's balance.
- Drill down to source transaction identifiers, status, currency, dates and policy/quotation link, subject to data-access scope.

## Scope and current-code risks

1. `reporting/services/product-performance-snapshot.service.ts` groups policy rows by currency but claim and invoice aggregates do not group by currency before joining; totals can be mixed or duplicated across policy-currency rows. Its claims and collections use current balances, not reconstructed historical balances. The report is not yet suitable for financial reliance.
2. `analytics/services/executive-metrics-source.service.ts` sums posted premium ledger rows and labels the result TZS without a currency predicate in the inspected method. It also resolves a user scope but the shown premium query filters company, not explicit branch. Treat its financial totals and scope as unverified until independently reviewed and reconciled.
3. The snapshot uses `policies.total_premium` for written premium and `POLICY`-sourced invoice paid amounts for collections. Existing quotation invoice creation uses `source_type: QUOTATION` (`billing-commission/services/quotation-invoice.service.ts`). Mapping and double-count prevention must be demonstrated before choosing either source.
4. `ReceiptAllocation` has allocation and reversal timestamps, while `FinanceRefund` has payment timestamps. Their presence alone does not establish complete, immutable historical movements. Audit creation, reversal, backdating and migration coverage before claiming historical reconstruction.
5. The current snapshot refresh deletes then inserts in separate data-source calls; transactional publication and recovery are future work, not established here.

## Illustration for discussion, not a business rule

Suppose a policy is issued for 1,000 in a single currency, the customer pays 600, and a later approved cancellation produces a 400 premium credit. The dashboard should show the **1,000 original written movement**, **600 collected**, and **400 cancellation credit** separately. It must not automatically report a 400 cash refund: Finance must determine how the credit affects the remaining receivable and whether any cash is actually due back. The reporting of net written premium and earned premium depends on the approved booking and cancellation rules.

## User-confirmed starting definitions — 6 November 2026

The user selected original transaction currencies without conversion, separate measures for posted receipts, premium allocations and paid refunds, and posting date for financial periods with effective date shown separately. These decisions provide the initial product direction; remaining recognition, treatment of tax, cancellation, claims and accounting-close questions in the decision table still need confirmation. No reconciliation against production data has been performed.

Step 3 now has isolated allocation and paid-refund cutoff helpers. They are not connected to reports and do not establish complete history, nor do they calculate written, earned, outstanding or Claims figures. A safe historical result requires complete company-scoped source rows and verified reversal history.
