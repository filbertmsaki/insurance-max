Correction report · Verification evidence · B16 scope and remaining gates

Root cause

  1. PolicyTransactionInvoiceService stored total additional payable as gross with hardcoded zero tax/fees and one bundled premium item.
  2. Endorsement repricing did not retain complete engine charge rule/category/base/effective-date evidence for invoice allocation.
  3. Repricing included a prior comparison snapshotHash before overwriting it, so the final unsigned snapshot hash was not reproducible.
