People and review rights

Current policy service blocks the maker subject or natural person from VALIDATE/APPROVE/ACTIVATE; it does not require validator and approver to be different from each other. Company approval rules may impose additional separation. One person cannot prepare and independently review the same version.

Account creation, Finance identity binding, READ, PREPARE_CONFIG/REVIEW_CONFIG, policy PREPARE/VALIDATE/APPROVE and ACTIVATE are distinct decisions. No generic USER/admin role assumed. No RELEASE grant just to obtain review access.

Existing seven-day READ grant expires 23 November 2026 at 12:09 EAT. Review before expiry; no automatic renewal.

Prevent duplicate accounting effects

LayerOwnership rule
OperationalQuotation/invoice billing records retain payable and shared tax breakdown. Billing memorandum does not itself create insurer IFRS 17 insurance service revenue. Cash executions, fees and taxes use their own approved events and single ownership.
IFRS 17Dedicated measurement records, assumptions, reproducible runs and approved result versions; Finance posts the approved movement exactly once. Reconcile provisional claims, insurance balances and held reinsurance component by component; never layer a full measured liability on an unreversed duplicate operational liability.
IFRS 9Only separately scoped financial assets/exposures outside the relevant IFRS 17 ownership; evidence for rights/discharge and impairment scope. No second premium receivable/ECL effect over the same IFRS 17 cash flows.
IAS 21Tag transaction/functional/presentation currency and rate evidence; assign FX ownership per exposure and measured component. Revaluation/translation and IFRS 17 finance movements must reconcile, not produce duplicate FX.
