# FIN-04 Accounts Receivable

Open **Finance → Accounts receivable** and select the company’s active primary accounting book. The workspace uses the existing customer, invoice, receipt, refund and general ledger records. Finance staff prepare and independently review customer documents. Transactions with accounting lines then enter the book’s GL approval queue. The final journal and customer balance changes commit in one transaction.

## Company setup

1. Activate the FIN-01 primary book, the FIN-02 live posting release and the required posting periods. Give preparers and independent reviewers scoped finance access and appropriate approval mandates.
2. In **New document**, prepare a **Receivables accounting policy** for each billing currency. Select account roles from the reviewed chart, enter the five loss percentages and choose reminder days. An empty reminder schedule disables delivery. Insurer books also select their premium cash control. Obtain independent approval.
3. Approve the effective debtor categories, credit limits, terms and payer mandates. Approve the currency policy before the first invoice on credit. Immediate-payment invoices can proceed under FIN-01. The policy defaults to requiring an approved profile for sales on credit. Finance can approve continued FIN-01 terms for unprofiled customers during migration. A zero limit permits immediate-payment billing. Insurance coverage and premium collection continue through the existing policy workflows.
4. Review the existing refund reservations and current balance exceptions. Finance can cancel outstanding refund requests, reconcile historical source records and certify opening currency allocations where needed. The migration rebuilds unapplied cash after historical paid refunds and preserves native transaction history.
5. For outgoing funds, configure the book’s existing bank observation verifier for the approved settlement account. The signed observation identifies the actual payee, document, amount, currency and bank reference.

## Bring forward unpaid external invoices

In **New document**, select **Import opening customer invoices**, the billing currency and the certified opening date. Use the CSV template with a customer code, external invoice reference, original invoice date, due date and unpaid amount on each row. The currency policy must be effective at cutover. In a multi-currency book, first certify the currency split of any nonzero AR control opening.

The invoice total plus the declared remainder must reconcile to the outstanding difference for that currency. For a large migration, submit successive batches of up to 5,000 invoices, enter the balance left for later batches and use zero for the final batch. Cumulative imports are limited to the certified opening. Independent approval registers collectible invoices and preserves their external references and due dates. They appear in the opening column of customer statements, the appropriate aging buckets and the ordinary receipts and collections workflows. The existing GL opening supplies the accounting balance. To replace an unused batch, submit **Correct an unused opening import** for review, then import the corrected file. The history retains both decisions.

## Daily use

Reverse a dependent credit note before reversing the debit note that supported it. If an older invoice has excess credit from a debit reversal, correct the credit note before applying or refunding the balance. The check covers the correction date and each later date with posted movements.

Prepare write-offs against the invoice exposure and allowance on the effective date. If a later assessment already posted, correct it through the reversal workflow on its original date in an open period before posting the earlier write-off and reassessing the later allowance.

A receipt, write-off or reversal that changes the payment status of an issued invoice uses the movement’s accounting period. Restoring an outstanding balance preserves the original invoice date, payment terms and due date. Migration `1800000000000-FinanceInvoiceBalancePeriods` applies this FIN-01 check to existing books.

- The invoice list and invoice detail show the percentage paid and outstanding relative to the original invoice amount. For example, receiving TZS 10,000,000 against TZS 15,000,000 shows 66.67% collected and 33.33% outstanding. Adjustments can also settle an invoice.
- The register shows the receipt’s payer and the invoice’s debtor separately. A posted advance protects that cash until a reviewed application or release. An invoice-linked refund returns an allocated collection and reopens the corresponding amount. A refund of unapplied cash consumes the remaining receipt balance.
- **Documents** shows the source, evidence, accounting lines and decision. A preparer submits an approved financial document for posting. **GL approvals** shows the accounting decision. A source-linked reversal records the correction and preserves the original journal. Reverse dependent applications before reversing their source.
- **Collections** shows outstanding invoices, contacts, follow-up history and delivered reminders. Retrying a dated follow-up or promise with its original request key returns the saved event. A changed date or promise amount requires a new action. Reminders go to the customer’s linked active portal inbox. The worker runs every five minutes and the same due-stage can also be run manually. Settled and disputed invoices are skipped. Customers without a linked portal account are visible for collector follow-up.
- **Statements and aging** reports each currency separately. Positive invoice balances fall into Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and Over 90. Undated invoices and unused credits have separate columns. The chart uses the totals for the selected customer, currency and reporting date. A third-party allocation and its reversal transfer balances between the payer and debtor within the same company. Both customer statements show their movements and the company control excludes the paired transfer when calculating insurer premium memoranda. The control report identifies differences and missing opening currency allocations for Finance to investigate. Statement approval compares dated invoice balances and source movements through the selected cutoff, including customer credits. A later discrepancy appears on its own effective date. Migration 1799900000000 reconstructs earlier balances from dated source records and records any difference from the live invoice balance on the migration date. Finance can investigate a cutover difference using the invoice reference and the native and reconstructed amounts.
