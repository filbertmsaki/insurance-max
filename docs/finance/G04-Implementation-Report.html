Full implementation and review report · Verification evidence · Workspace and isolated rehearsal guide

What the controls enforce

  • Current authenticated membership, tenant, legal entity, book, exact identity and separate scoped authority on every operation; no platform-admin fallback.
  • Different natural people prepare and review, even across multiple logins. Company-admin role is required for legal, appointment, accounting-basis, policy and completion decisions.
  • Immutable versions and chained events; exact content hashes and latest dependencies; changed tax catalogue or unavailable evidence invalidates completion.
  • Only owned available document versions can be downloaded; bytes and SHA-256 must match before delivery; native document access audit is recorded.
  • 13 configuration families reuse existing services. Tax rules pin existing effective-dated catalogues and preserve customer payable; no second tax calculator.
  • Operational memos, financial movements and IFRS measurement ownership remain separate. Setup posting rules are disabled; no setup activation endpoint exists.
  • New company draft creation is explicitly recorded as automated preparation under the user's authorization, never a human signature or approval.
  • The notification header now respects notification permission and scopes its cache to the current membership/session.

Remaining actual-company requirements

  1. Supply actual regulatory/tax/registration evidence and valid dates.
  2. Confirm the nominated people and independently review the formal appointment.
  3. Make actual accounting, currency, calendar, chart, tax, source and policy elections with supporting evidence.
  4. Complete independent company review of all ten exact versions; refresh stale/expired evidence and mandates.
  5. Policy activation, posting, operational migration, B15, cutover and physical service extraction remain separate work/gates.

Verification and practical limits

Both application builds and final regression checks passed. Migration down refuses deletion once any setup evidence exists. Forward correction uses new immutable versions. Empty-schema rollback retains permission catalogue entries to avoid revoking access.
