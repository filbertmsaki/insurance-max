# FIN-05 Fixed Assets Management

Open **Finance → Fixed assets** and select the company’s active primary book. Finance preparers submit evidence-backed documents. An independent reviewer approves them. Accounting entries appear in **GL approvals** after the preparer submits the approved journal. Posting the live journal updates the register in the same database transaction.

| 1. Setup | .01 Categories, .02 groups, .03 locations, .04 depreciation configuration | Approve groups and branch locations, then a dated category. Choose the valuation model, useful life, method and posting accounts. Each asset keeps the approved accounting mappings. |

## Set up the book

1. Activate the FIN-01 primary book and FIN-02 live posting. Open the accounting periods to be used. Assign prepare, independent review and GL approval mandates in the functional currency.
2. Map dedicated asset cost, clearing, accumulated depreciation, impairment, expense and gain roles from the reviewed chart. Add revaluation surplus and retained earnings for a revaluation class, and liability and interest roles for a leased class. The manual voucher component must authorize the posting sides and the account dimensions. A category revision applies to future acquisitions. An existing asset keeps its accounts when Finance changes a category.
3. Create approved asset locations linked to eligible branches. Enter the asset’s other operating dimensions at acquisition. A transfer moves recognized balances between dimensions. Configure inter-branch clearing roles for transfers between branches so both branches balance.
4. For assets already in service at cutover, use **Register opening balance** with the certified opening date, original in-service date, gross cost, accumulated depreciation and residual value. Where applicable, include opening impairment, revaluation surplus and the remaining lease payments at the certified opening date. The total allocated to each account must fit its certified debit or credit. Review any remaining ledger differences before sign-off.

## Operate the register

- **Acquisition and capitalization.** Select the purchase journal and line, including a FIN-03 supplier bill journal. Several assets can share a line up to its available functional value. Capitalization transfers clearing to asset cost. A purchase already debited to the asset account and dimensions needs only the register entry. Reverse the linked asset event before reversing its funding journal.
- **Depreciation.** Straight-line and declining-balance schedules start when the asset is available for use. A non-depreciating category supports land. Partial calendar months are prorated. The final charge preserves residual value and absorbs rounding. A TZS 1,200,000 laptop with no residual and a 48-month life produces TZS 25,000 for a full month and TZS 300,000 for a full year. The calculator shows book depreciation. Tax schedules are separate.
- **Valuation and loss.** Submit the valuation for every active asset in a revalued class under one valuation reference. Finish prior monthly charges before a valuation, transfer or retirement. The register records reserve and profit-or-loss movements and limits an impairment recovery to the carrying amount that would have applied without the loss.
- **Custody.** An assignment identifies an active company user. An inspection records found, missing or damaged status with evidence. Finance reviews any resulting write-off separately. Paid maintenance references a posted expense line so the asset history carries the cost once.
- **Leases.** Enter payments due at the end of each monthly lease period in the functional currency. Supply a payment list for variable rents or rent-free months. Enter the nominal annual discount rate and any evidenced net commencement adjustment. Each instalment displays opening liability, interest, payment and closing liability. Accrue interest and link settled payments to the posted settlement line. Partial payments retain the unpaid balance. Remeasure the liability and the right-of-use asset on the effective modification date. Enter the next contractual due date for a short first period. Unpaid rentals stay due unless the approved amendment includes them in the revised payment stream. On early termination, record the agreement and any settled fee. An approved short-term or low-value exemption records rental expense against its posted source.
- **Corrections and reports.** Reverse the latest affected asset event and then enter the replacement. The original and reversal remain visible. Investigate control-account differences against the certified opening, asset registrations and other GL postings. Inspect prior register snapshots by changing the report end date.

The accounting design draws on the recognition and measurement principles for owned assets, impairment and lessee accounting. Finance approves each company’s estimates, classifications and exemptions.
