# Missing modules and capabilities

Absence is based on repository discovery, registered Finance modules, route inventory, and related source modules. These are implementation gaps, not a claim that the business scope has been approved.

## Foundation

No capability classified as wholly missing.

## Accounting

- **FIN-16 Recurring Journals:** Build scheduled recurring journal templates, approvals, execution, and reversal policy.
- **FIN-17 Accruals and Prepayments:** Build governed accrual and amortization schedules with period posting.
- **FIN-19 Intercompany Accounting:** Build paired company entries, due-to/due-from, elimination, and reconciliation.

## Receivables

- **FIN-29 Credit and Debit Notes:** Build governed credit/debit note documents and tax/ledger effects.
- **FIN-32 Installment Management:** Build installment schedules, due dates, allocation, arrears, and policy consequences.
- **FIN-33 Broker and Agent Receivables:** Build intermediary subledger, statements, allocation, and settlement reconciliation.

## Payables

- **FIN-35 Supplier Management:** Build supplier master with beneficiary validation, approvals, and change audit.
- **FIN-36 Supplier Invoices:** Build invoice capture, duplicate checks, matching, approvals, and AP posting.
- **FIN-37 Expense Management:** Build expense coding, evidence, approval, tax, and posting.
- **FIN-38 Payment Vouchers:** Build numbered vouchers linked to approved obligations and payment evidence.
- **FIN-40 Petty Cash:** Build custodian balances, imprest replenishment, receipts, and reconciliation.
- **FIN-41 Employee Reimbursements:** Build claims, evidence, approval, beneficiary checks, and payment.
- **FIN-42 Budget Management:** Build versioned budgets by period, company, branch, and dimension.
- **FIN-43 Budgetary Controls:** Build commitment and spend checks against approved budgets.
- **FIN-44 Fixed Assets:** Build asset register, capitalization, depreciation, disposal, and GL tie-out.
- **FIN-45 Procurement Finance:** Build purchase-order matching, commitments, accruals, and supplier settlement.

## Insurance accounting

- **FIN-55 Reinsurance Statements:** Build treaty/facultative statements with balances, cash calls, and counterpart confirmation.
- **FIN-56 Insurance Technical Balances:** Build premium, claims, reserve, commission, and ceded technical balance rollforwards.

## IFRS 17

- **FIN-58 IFRS 17 Configuration:** Build approved IFRS 17 policies, portfolios, methods, assumptions, and disclosures.
- **FIN-59 Contract Classification:** Build insurance contract and investment component classification.
- **FIN-60 Contract Grouping:** Build portfolios, annual cohorts, and profitability groups.
- **FIN-61 Measurement Models:** Build GMM, PAA, and VFA eligibility and measurement.
- **FIN-62 Insurance Cash Flows:** Build fulfilment cash-flow projections and experience adjustments.
- **FIN-63 Discount Rate Management:** Build governed yield curves, locked rates, and sensitivity history.
- **FIN-64 Risk Adjustment:** Build risk adjustment methodology, calculation, and disclosure.
- **FIN-65 Contractual Service Margin:** Build initial CSM, rollforward, and coverage-unit release.
- **FIN-66 Liability for Remaining Coverage:** Build LRC measurement and reconciliation.
- **FIN-67 Liability for Incurred Claims:** Build LIC measurement including discounting and risk adjustment.
- **FIN-68 Loss Components:** Build onerous group loss component recognition and reversal.
- **FIN-69 Reinsurance Held Measurement:** Build reinsurance held groups and recovery components.
- **FIN-70 IFRS 17 Accounting Engine:** Build subledger journal generation and audited reconciliation.
- **FIN-71 IFRS 17 Reporting:** Build IFRS 17 rollforwards, disclosures, and statement mapping.

## Treasury

- **FIN-72 Bank Account Management:** Build scoped bank account master, signatories, and account validation.
- **FIN-76 Treasury Management:** Build liquidity positions, transfers, limits, and approval controls.
- **FIN-77 Cash Flow Forecasting:** Build forecast drivers, horizons, and variance tracking.
- **FIN-78 Investment Accounting:** Build investment positions, income, impairment, and ledger events.
- **FIN-79 Investment Valuation:** Build valuation inputs, fair-value hierarchy, and controls.
- **FIN-81 Bank Integration:** Build signed bank statement and payment transport adapters with reconciliation.

## Taxation

- **FIN-85 Withholding Tax:** Build withholding rules, certificates, remittance, and ledger reporting.
- **FIN-86 Corporate Income Tax:** Build current/deferred tax, computations, and return support.
- **FIN-89 Fiscal Documents:** Build fiscal document numbering, issuance, corrections, and TRA integration as applicable.
- **FIN-90 Tax Reconciliation:** Build tax subledger-to-GL and return reconciliation.

## Reporting

- **FIN-93 Income Statement:** Build governed P&L mapping, comparative periods, and drilldown.
- **FIN-94 Balance Sheet:** Build position statement mapping and balancing controls.
- **FIN-95 Cash Flow Statement:** Build IAS 7 cash-flow classification and reconciliation.
- **FIN-96 Statement of Changes in Equity:** Build equity movement rollforward and comparative statement.
- **FIN-97 Financial Statement Notes:** Build disclosure schedules and source traceability.
- **FIN-100 Solvency and Capital Monitoring:** Build capital resources, requirements, thresholds, and evidence.
- **FIN-101 Liquidity Reporting:** Build liquidity measures and stress views tied to cash/investments.
- **FIN-102 Financial Consolidation:** Build entity consolidation, FX translation, and eliminations.

## Governance

- **FIN-115 Financial Close Monitoring:** Build close status dashboard, exceptions, overdue tasks, and signoff evidence.
